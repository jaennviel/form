About FORM

Clothes with character.

FORM is a designer archive concept store, bringing together clothing with distinctive silhouettes, unexpected materials and considered construction.

The collection moves between minimal utility and experimental shapes. Each of its ten pieces offers a different point of view, with a short description, a design note and a credited image source.
